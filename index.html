About Us

For over four decades, the Okoli name has been synonymous with dependable road transport in Nigeria.

From the mid-1970s, our founders C. N. Okoli Transport Company Nigeria LTD contributed to connecting people, goods and communities across states through a legacy of safe, structured, and customer-focused operations.

That foundation built the discipline, logistics expertise, and deep understanding of Nigeria's transport realities that power our journey today.

Mission:
Make clean mobility profitable, accessible and empowering.

Vision:
Help lead Africa's transition into electric transportation.

Why Choose Us:
40+ years transport heritage combined with EV innovation.
